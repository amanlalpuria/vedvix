# Instagram profile setup

The copy and images for [instagram.com/vedvix2](https://www.instagram.com/vedvix2/). Images are in `posters/out/` and their source is [`posters/profile.html`](posters/profile.html). To re-render them, run `node marketing/social/posters/render.mjs pf hl`.

## Profile picture

| File | Look | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `pf-dark.png` | White V with a blue diamond on navy, with a soft blue glow | **Recommended.** Matches the website and the post designs. |
| `pf-blue.png` | White V on brand blue | Stands out more in the stories bar and search results. Use it if the dark one looks too subtle next to other accounts. |

Both are 1080×1080 with the mark centred, so nothing is lost when Instagram crops them to a circle. Use the logo mark only, not the "vedvix" wordmark: at 110px wide, text is unreadable.

## Name field

```text
Vedvix | App & Web Development
```

Instagram search matches the **Name** field as well as the username, so add the keywords people search for here. Other options: `Vedvix | Product Studio`, `Vedvix | Apps · SaaS · AI`.

## Links

Instagram allows up to 5 links. Add them in this order (the first one shows on the profile):

1. `https://vedvix.in`: title **Website**
2. `https://vedvix.in/work`: title **Our work**
3. `https://linkedin.com/company/vedvix`: title **LinkedIn**
